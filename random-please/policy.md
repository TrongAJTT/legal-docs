# Privacy Policy for Random Please

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

**1. No Personal Data Collection**

Random Please is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We explicitly state that **we do not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or persistent device identifiers.**

**2. Local Processing & Storage**

All core functionalities—including coin flips, dice rolls, number calling, password generation, list picking, and other randomizations—are computed entirely on your device. Your settings, customized presets, and interaction history are stored solely in your device's local storage (`SharedPreferences` / `Hive`) and never leave your device.

**3. Internet Access**

The application functions completely offline. Internet access is only utilized when explicitly triggered by the user for optional operations:
- Fetching community or public list templates from predefined or user-configured URLs.

External links (such as project documentation or release notes on GitHub) open externally in your device's default web browser. No personal data, user content, or device identifiers are ever collected or sent to external servers.

**4. Third-Party Services & Tracking**

Random Please operates independently. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, user profiling tools, or external crash reporting services that collect user information.

**5. Children's Privacy**

Because Random Please does not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy regulations.

**6. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or new features in the application. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
